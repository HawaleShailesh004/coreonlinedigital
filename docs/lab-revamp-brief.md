## Reference ranking for Coreline

| Site | What it is | Fit for us | Steal | Skip |
|------|------------|------------|-------|------|
| **[Resend](https://resend.com/)** | Dark SaaS, kinetic product marketing | **Best overall** | Hero as product artifact, glow accents, section rhythm, motion that serves clarity | Pure “for developers” tone |
| **[AuthKit](https://www.authkit.com/)** | Cinematic dark, glass product cards | **Best visual voltage** | Spotlit wordmark, floating glass panels, glow-not-fill accent | Blue/violet palette (use emerald) |
| **[Zero to One](https://zerotoonebrand.in/)** | Mumbai full-stack growth studio | **Best business model twin** | Multi-service “one team” pitch, chatbot-as-proof, measurable outcomes | Generic “six disciplines” card layout |
| **[Estrela](https://estrela.studio/)** | People-first design studio | **Best voice / craft** | Honesty, featured work as narrative, people-first close | “We don’t develop” (we do) |
| **[Flammini](https://www.flammini.design/)** | Designer portfolio craft | Strong for About/Work | Typography, spacing, case framing | Too portfolio-only for SMB sales |
| **[Marckuiper](https://www.marckuiper.com/)** | Personal creative site | Medium | Personality, motion taste | Not multi-service |
| **[Satellitor](https://satellitor.com/)** | Product / spatial feel | Medium | Spatial sections, modern dark UI | Product-SaaS framing |
| **[MultiversX](https://multiversx.com/)** | Crypto ecosystem dark | Low for buyers | Scroll depth, tech swagger | Crypto culture, complexity |
| **[Lusion](https://lusion.co/)** | WebGL creative studio ceiling | **Aspirational only** | Confidence, loading ritual, “experience = brand” | Full WebGL cost, slow on cheap phones, wrong for Thane cold traffic as *default* |

### Verdict — what we build toward

1. **Primary language:** Resend + AuthKit (dark, kinetic, product-on-canvas) with **Coreline emerald** instead of blue/violet.  
2. **Business spine:** Zero to One (full online growth stack, one owner).  
3. **Human craft:** Estrela (honest, people-first).  
4. **Not day-one:** Lusion-level WebGL — optional later as a `/experience` Easter egg, not the homepage for Google traffic.
