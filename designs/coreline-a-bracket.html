Questions people ask before they say yes

Do I have to pay everything upfront?

Half to start, half when the site goes live. If you are not happy with the design at day three, you can walk away and keep the deposit as credit.

What if I do not have photos or content?

Most businesses do not. I write the copy from our call and use licensed photography until you have your own. Content is the reason projects run late, so I remove that dependency.

Who owns the website?

You do. Domain, hosting, code, all in your name from day one. If you stop working with me you take everything with you.

Do you actually build the AI agents yourself?

Yes. They run on your own content, not a generic FAQ widget. You can talk to one on this site before you buy.

You should not, on faith. Open the work above on your phone, check how fast it loads, and call one of the owners. That is a better test than a team photo.
