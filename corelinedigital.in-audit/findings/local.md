## Findings

### 1. No Google Business Profile signal - CRITICAL

No `sameAs` link to a GBP listing, no embedded map, no review content. For a
local service business, GBP is the single largest source of qualified local
traffic and the prerequisite for map-pack visibility. Nothing else in this
report will outperform claiming and completing it.

### 2. No reviews anywhere - CRITICAL

Zero reviews on-site or referenced. Review count and velocity are dominant
local-pack ranking factors. Note the irony worth resolving: the services page
sells _"Automated review requests after every booking or sale"_ while the
business itself displays none.

### 3. No location or service-area landing pages - HIGH

No page targets "web design Thane", "website developer Thane West", or
"website design Mumbai". The site relies on four generic pages to rank for
geo-modified commercial queries. Note: build these as genuinely distinct pages
with local specifics - thin templated location pages are a liability.

### 4. Location absent from live titles and body copy - HIGH

Covered in `onpage.md` and `content.md`. "Thane" appears in live body text only
in the footer address.

### 5. No citations or directory listings - MEDIUM

No evidence of JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Google Maps, or LinkedIn presence.
Citation consistency is a core local ranking factor.

### 6. Address is partial - MEDIUM

"Wagle Estate" is an industrial estate, not a street address. GBP verification
will require a full, precise address. Confirm whether the business is
address-visible or service-area-only, and set the GBP listing accordingly.
