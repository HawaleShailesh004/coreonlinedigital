## What works

- The copy is genuinely well written. Specific, concrete, confident, and free of
  the generic AI-agency register ("leverage synergies…"). Lines like "A missed
  call at 9pm is a lost customer by 9:05" are strong, memorable, and quotable.
- Clear differentiated positioning (systems vs. templates) sustained across pages.
- Real specificity in service descriptions - WhatsApp routing, booking calendars,
  follow-up sequences - not vague capability lists.

## Findings

### 1. No content engine at all - CRITICAL

Four pages, no blog, no guides, no case studies. There is nothing on the site
targeting informational or long-tail queries - the queries that actually build
topical authority for a local agency. Every page is bottom-of-funnel.

### 2. Zero geo/commercial keyword presence in body copy - CRITICAL

"Thane" appears in live body text only in the footer address line. The site
never says what it is in the words its buyers use: _website design_, _web
developer_, _website company_, paired with _Thane_, _Wagle Estate_, _Mumbai_.
The positioning language ("digital infrastructure", "systems") is distinctive
but has effectively no search volume.

The local `seoKeywords` list already identifies the right 15 terms. The body
copy still needs to earn them.

### 3. E-E-A-T signals are minimal - HIGH

- Founder is "Shailesh" - first name only. No surname, no bio, no photo, no
  credentials, no LinkedIn, no years of experience.
- No `sameAs` links anywhere on the live site; no social profiles in the footer.
- No named clients, no testimonials, no reviews, no logos.
- No published pricing live (the local tree has ₹15,000 - deploy it).
- No About page live (exists locally, 404 in production).

For a single-operator agency, the operator _is_ the trust signal. Right now
that signal is one paragraph and a first name.

### 4. Portfolio is samples, not client work - HIGH (positioning risk)

The homepage section is headed "Recent builds / A few systems we've built",
while `/work` describes the same items as "Ten industry sample builds" and
"Built to show what's possible." A prospect who reads both will notice.

Credit where due: the working tree already contains an FAQ entry -
_"Are the sites on the Work page real clients?"_ - which addresses this
head-on. That is the right call. Deploy it, and align the homepage heading so
the two pages tell the same story.

### 5. Contact page has no subheadings - MEDIUM

`/contact` has an H1 and no H2 or H3 at all. Add structure (Visit / Call /
WhatsApp / What happens next) for both accessibility and passage extraction.

### 6. No FAQ content live - MEDIUM

`faqJsonLd()` and five written FAQs exist in the working tree, unshipped. FAQs
are the single most citable content format for AI search. Ship them.
