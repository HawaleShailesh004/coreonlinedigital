The “missing” loop

Idle animation for the hero. The words disappear into the background, then come back. The page performs the sentence while nobody is scrolling. Three ways to do it — pick one.

A · Sweep erase

A soft band travels across and wipes the words away, then reveals them again on the return. Uses a moving mask gradient. Cheapest and the most deliberate-looking.

Is missing

B · Dissolve, letter by letter

Each letter fades and blurs on its own offset, so the phrase comes apart and reassembles. Most literal reading of “missing”. Blur is the expensive part — cap it at 6px.

Is missing

C · Sink into the background

The whole phrase fades almost to nothing and shrinks very slightly, as if it dropped behind the page, then returns. Quietest of the three, and the safest on slow phones.
