# Sample Build 3 - CA / Professional Services

Vertical: Chartered Accountant, tax/audit consultant, or similar professional practice
Purpose: Portfolio sample for Coreline's Work page + cold-call demo asset for CA/legal/consulting leads.

---

## 1. Positioning

This site sells competence and reliability above everything - a CA's client is trusting them with money and compliance, so nothing here should feel casual, trendy, or "designed." The safest-feeling site in the entire portfolio should be this one.

---

## 2. Copy

### Nav

Logo/firm name · Services · About · Contact
CTA button: **Book a Consultation**

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** Chartered Accountants · [City]

**Headline:** Your filings, audits, and compliance - handled before the deadline worries you.

**Sub:** [Firm Name] manages tax filing, GST, audits, and financial planning for individuals and small businesses, with direct access to the person handling your case - not a call center.

**CTAs:** Book a Consultation · View Services

### Services (list format, not cards)

Clean vertical list, not flashy grid - this audience wants information density over visual flair:

- **Income Tax Filing** - Individual and business returns, planning included.
- **GST Registration & Filing** - Monthly/quarterly compliance handled end-to-end.
- **Audit & Assurance** - Statutory and internal audits for small and mid-size businesses.
- **Financial & Tax Planning** - Advisory to reduce liability legally, planned ahead of deadlines.

### Why Us

**Heading:** Direct access, not a queue.

3-point strip:

- Work directly with the CA on your case, not a junior handoff
- Deadline reminders sent automatically - nothing missed
- Documents shared securely, tracked in one place

### About

**Heading:** About [Name], CA

2–3 sentences: qualification (CA/ICAI membership), years in practice, specialization if any (e.g. small business, real estate, NRI taxation). Photo: professional headshot, formal.

### Contact

Office address, map, phone, WhatsApp, office hours. Simple form: Name, phone, service needed (dropdown).

**Bottom CTA:** Don't wait for the deadline to find a CA. Book a consultation today.

## 3. Design System

### Palette - "Ledger Navy"

```
--bg:      #FFFFFF   /* pure white - nothing softened, this audience reads white as "official" */
--ink:     #12203A   /* deep navy-black, the one vertical where navy is the right, not lazy, choice */
--primary: #1E3A6E   /* trust navy - used for CTAs and headers */
--accent:  #B8934A   /* muted gold, used only for small credential/badge accents (e.g. "ICAI Member") */
--grey:    #5B6472
--hair:    rgba(18,32,58,0.12)
```

Navy is the one place in this whole portfolio where the "obvious corporate blue" choice is actually correct - this audience's trust signals are genuinely conservative, and this is the vertical to play it straight rather than differentiate for its own sake.

### Typography

- **Display:** A plain, sturdy sans - Inter (semi-bold) or IBM Plex Sans - no personality, no flourish. This is intentional; a CA site with a "designed" typeface undermines trust rather than building it.
- **Body:** Same family as display, regular weight, comfortable line-height (1.6).
- **No mono, no serif, no display flourishes anywhere** - the entire site should read like a well-organized document, because that's what this client is actually being sold: organization.

### Spacing & Feel

- Sharp corners, minimal shadows, mostly flat.
- Information-dense but organized - more text per section than the other two samples, structured in clear lists/tables rather than large visual blocks.
- Use a subtle document/ledger-line texture as a background motif if anything decorative is needed at all - ties to the "your filings organized" theme without being literal or gimmicky.

### Motion

- Minimal. Fade-up on scroll, 150ms hovers, nothing else.
- No parallax, no gradient sweeps, no marquees - a CA's client should never feel like they're on a flashy site; flashy directly undermines the sell here.
- The one animation worth having: a small animated checkmark or tick sequence next to the "Why Us" strip points, appearing in order as the section scrolls into view - reinforces "things get done, on time, in order."

- This is the most conservative site in the portfolio by design - resist the urge to make it "interesting." Competence, not personality, is the product.
