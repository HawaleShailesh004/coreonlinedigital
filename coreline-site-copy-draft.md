## Copy notes for the build

- Every CTA button text should stay consistent site-wide: "Book Strategy Call" is the primary action everywhere - never rename it "Contact Us," "Get Started," etc. on different pages. Consistency here matters more than variety.
- No pricing numbers appear anywhere in this copy. Every service section ends in "Get a quote" / "Book Strategy Call."
- No testimonials, no client counts, no "trusted by" claims - none exist yet. Nothing above is fabricated proof.
